# Modeling gaps

What this corpus does not yet model, and why. There are two lists because there are two reasons.

**Read the first list as a statement about our understanding, not about the standard.** We are not URPX experts. Most things that looked unexpressible in this corpus turned out to be patterns we had missed. Expect more of the list below to go the same way. An entry leaves that list two ways: URPX gains the vocabulary, or we learn how to say it with what is already there.

Discussion #37 is the example. It assumed a sum of per-account demand could not be stated. The reviewer asked what `urpx:ConditionExpression` could not do here, we tried it, and it validated. What survived was a narrower claim: nothing tells a consumer which accounts the sum ranges over.

So before anything is added below, build the minimal document and run `pyshacl` against the shipped shapes. "We cannot express this" and "we have not worked out how to express this" are different claims.

[README](./README.md) describes what the corpus *does* contain. Each filing's `tariff.toml [modeling].deferrals` records its own. This file is the corpus-wide view.

`dev/lint_gaps_agree.py` checks that the two still agree: every claim in the first list must name a filing that exists, and at least one deferral in a filing it names must assert an upstream gap. The two had disagreed for two days before anything compared them.

## We have not found a way to express these in URPX v0.5.1

Each entry gives the upstream thread, what the shapes refuse, and where in the corpus it bites.

- [**urpx#10**](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx/issues/10): `urpx:ProgramTypeValue` is a closed eight-value enumeration with no extension point, so a utility-minted category does not validate even when bridged to a shipped value with `skos:broader`, and `ProgramEnrollmentShape` is `sh:closed`, so no annotation can be hung on the enrollment either. Four kinds of program in this corpus have no value: senior citizen / disability (LADWP Lifeline, SCE Medical Baseline), deed-restricted affordable housing (SCE Deed-Restricted, SDG&E DRAH), critical peak pricing (SCE PRIME-CPP, SMUD CPP; price exposure without a curtailment obligation, so not `demandResponseProgram`), and supply arrangements (PG&E E-1's Direct Access and Transitional Bundled Service enrollments carry `urpx:programName` with no `urpx:programType`). The seven modifier filings for those programs model no `urpx:ProgramEnrollment` at all.
- [**urpx-org/urpx discussion #32**](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx/discussions/32): `urpx:ServiceBracket` carries absolute `lowerBound` / `upperBound` only and `ServiceBracketShape` is `sh:closed`, so an additive baseline allowance cannot appear as data anywhere in the graph; the shift has to be precomputed into an absolute bound for every (territory, code, season) cell, including in a modifier overlay that supplies its own `ServiceTiers`. Blocks PG&E medical baseline Code M / Code S (+16.438 kWh/day) and SCE HPWH (+1.9 kWh/day summer, +2.6/3.0 kWh/day winter for HPWH-attesting TOU-D customers). Neither modifier is modeled here, which is the effect. The two filings that do carry baseline structure, PG&E E-1 (6 `urpx:ServiceBracket`) and the E-TOU-C baseline-tier modifier (120), can state absolute bounds only, so an additive allowance would have to be precomputed into every cell of both.
- [**urpx-org/urpx discussion #36**](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx/discussions/36): a Standby Charge's **conditional demand credit** has no structured form. CPAU E-7 Section D.7.d.(1) reduces Maximum Demand by the generation of any non-utility generator that was not running at the peak, floored at zero. `OperationNode` is reachable only inside a `ConditionExpression`, which yields a boolean; `CalculationMethodShape` is `sh:closed` over nine properties with no offset or netting path; `AggregationMethodValue` has five members and none nets one quantity against another; and nothing floors a result at zero. The **exemption** half of this thread is not a gap: a `urpx:standbyCharge` Price gated by `urpx:hasApplicabilityRule` conforms, which the four CPAU filings recorded on 2026-09-21. It is below.
- [**urpx-org/urpx discussion #37**](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx/discussions/37): `urpx:aggregationMethod` on a `urpx:CalculationMethod` states the operation, but nothing names the set it ranges over, and v0.5.1 has no site or account class (`urpx:AccountServiceAgreement` records that service account data lives in customer data rather than in URPX), so a sum of per-account monthly maximum demand cannot be scoped to the accounts at one site. Bites CPAU E-7 and E-7 TOU, which defer Section D.3 site aggregation to prose.
- [**urpx-org/urpx discussion #38**](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx/discussions/38): URPX names no canonical baseline-allowance metric, so each publisher mints its own denominator for a percent-of-baseline condition and nothing tells a consumer that PG&E's and SDG&E's denote the same quantity. The unit itself is not the gap: `urpx:percent` and `urpx:ratio` are `UnitOfMeasureValue` members and `measurementKind` is optional on `MetricSpecificationShape`, so the semantics can live on the referenced `MetricSpecification` rather than in prose. PG&E EV2's own manifest records that the older attribution to a missing percent unit was wrong as written.

## Not yet modeled

Tariffs and modifiers this corpus has not converted. Some are buildable today and need only the work; others are held on something, and each entry says which. The heading used to read "Expressible, not yet modeled", which was false for the three entries below that name a blocker: two are held on upstream vocabulary and one on the consumer.

Tracked in [Issues](https://github.com/grid-coordination/urpx-rate-plans/issues). Highlights:

- **LADWP NEM export credit** ([discussion #63](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx/discussions/63)). Service Rider NEM Section 3.b compensates exported energy "using the applicable Rate Schedule energy pricing", a phrase that occurs once in the 88-page ordinance and that the ordinance does not define. Three readings survive the source: the Energy Charge line alone, $0.07142 per kWh on `ladwp:r-1a` at the April 2026 factors; that charge plus the seven per-kWh adjustments, $0.24362; and the Standard Energy Credit at estimated hourly marginal production cost, which Schedule R-1 Section 4.b directs parallel generators to and the rider never displaces. The readings need different shapes rather than different numbers, so no cell is buildable: the first is 18 credit Prices each naming one `urpx:basedOnPrice`, a shape measured to work, and the second cannot use it because several `basedOnPrice` entries are read as alternatives rather than summed. What would settle it is one redacted R-1A net-metering bill for a month whose factors this corpus holds. SMUD NEM1's twelve cells are built.
- **CPAU standby-charge exemptions** ([discussion #36](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx/discussions/36)). The on-site-generation exemption conforms at v0.5.1 as a `urpx:standbyCharge` Price gated by `urpx:hasApplicabilityRule`, verified by pyshacl on 2026-09-21 and recorded in each of the four CPAU manifests. Unbuilt because the pinned clj-urpx does not evaluate a `ConditionExpression`, so the gate would be inert and the charge would apply to every customer.
- SCE TOU-D HPWH (Heat Pump Water Heater) baseline-allocation modifier and PG&E Code M / Code S medical baseline modeling, both blocked on [urpx-org/urpx discussion #32](https://github.com/urpx-org/urpx/discussions/32) (additive baseline allowance vocabulary). Three CA IOUs hitting the same shape; the +1.9 / +2.6 / +3.0 kWh/day SCE HPWH extension structure documented in SCE Preliminary Statement Part H; URPX vocabulary path TBD.
- PG&E CARE/FERA modifiers: same shape as the SCE and SDG&E modifiers, which now carry both the line-item exemptions and the headline percentage discount, so the pattern to follow is established; deferred for utility-coverage breadth.
- SMUD EAPR income-tier usage discount: the program gives a 100 percent reduction in usage cost capped at $60, $32, $10 or $0 per month by Federal Poverty Level tier. URPX v0.5.1 can express a capped credit as marginal `urpx:ServiceBracket`s over a money-denominated `urpx:MetricInput`, with a -1.0 multiplier on the first bracket and 0.0 on the remainder, and that construction validates. What it cannot state is that a bracket bound of 60 means sixty dollars: `urpx:ServiceBracket` bounds carry no unit, and `DatatypeOperandNodeShape` is `sh:closed` with `urpx:unitOfMeasure` attaching only to a `MetricSpecification`. The $10/month fixed-charge discount is modeled exactly.
- LADWP REO (Renewable Energy Option): REAF dollar value not published in the tariff ordinance (formula only); needs source-discovery for an LADWP annual REA report before modeling. Tracked as a closed-deferred issue; re-file if the value is published.
- Smaller-utility coverage (Imperial Irrigation District, City of Anaheim, Modesto Irrigation District, Riverside, Glendale, etc.): each adds ~50K–150K residential customers and broadens statewide coverage.
