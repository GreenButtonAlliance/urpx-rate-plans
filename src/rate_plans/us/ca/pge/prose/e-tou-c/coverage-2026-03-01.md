Five parts of the published Schedule E-TOU-C are not represented in this filing. The Special Condition 8 bill protection, which for twelve months after enrollment credits the customer the difference between what it paid on this schedule and what it would have paid on the tiered non-time-of-use Schedule E-1, with its own exclusions (customers on Schedules NEM2, NEM2V or NEM2VMSH who start or transfer service on or after October 1, 2020, customers opting in from another time-of-use plan, and customers requesting enrollment after May 4, 2022) and a parallel twelve-month version on Sheet 9 covering the non-generation charges of community choice aggregation and direct access customers. The CARE half of the Wildfire Fund Charge exemption: Special Condition 11 excludes CARE and medical baseline sales from the charge, and the medical baseline half is pge:e-tou-c-medical-baseline-{date}, but pge:d-care's exemption names the 2026-03-27 version's Price, so a CARE customer under this version is priced ${wildfire-fund} per kWh. The Sheet 4 unbundling of the base services charge into its Distribution, Public Purpose Program, Nuclear Decommissioning and New System Generation components for each income tier; only the three daily totals ({bsc-and}) are modeled. The second qualification path for Income Tier 2, an affordable rental unit in a property listed in the California Housing Partnership's database and restricted to residents at or below 80 percent of Area Median Income; only the FERA path is modeled, so a customer qualifying through affordable housing alone resolves to the Tier 3 charge. And the Sheet 1 instruction that time-of-use period usage be assigned to baseline tiers pro rata, so that a customer with twenty percent of its usage in the peak period has twenty percent of the usage in each tier treated as on-peak: this filing carries both the time-of-use dimension and the baseline tiers, but nothing states how the two combine.

Three things are out of scope rather than missing. The standby reservation charges the Applicability section imports from Schedule S Special Conditions 1 through 6 for customers partly supplied from a nonutility source are charges of another schedule, and with them go the Special Condition 9 and 10 exemptions for sub-megawatt non-exporting solar and for distributed energy resources. Special Condition 6's designation of a primary meter and additional meters, and Special Condition 7's one-time option for a separately metered common-area account to move to a general service schedule, are customer-service procedures and not rates.
