Rule 19 Part F's Additional Medical Baseline Quantities, which PG&E assigns case by case in increments of {quantity} kWh per day, are not carried: this version adds one Standard Medical Baseline Quantity. The offsets are kWh per day, as are the bounds they adjust, and this document does not state how a consumer scales either to a billing period. The adjusting brackets name the tier @ids of {base}, so a later version of the schedule needs its own version of this modifier. A customer on both CARE and Medical Baseline is exempt from the Wildfire Fund Charge once, but pge:d-care and this modifier each carry a -1 multiple of the same Price, so a composition holding both would credit it twice; the one-modifier composition policy in this repository never builds that composition.
