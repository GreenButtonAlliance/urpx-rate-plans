Auto-generated by `dev/list_california_parties.py` from the mined CEC MIDAS feed in `data/midas/`. Do not edit by hand.

**Every count below is as of 2026-09-23**. MIDAS is a live feed: parties appear, rename and withdraw, and identifier counts move daily.

20 organizations sell electricity here: 5 have filings in this corpus and 15 have a directory and a discovery manifest only. 2 further rows are signals MIDAS carries that are not rates and have no seller, so they have no directory. Per-segment identifier counts and document links are in [midas-catalog.md](./midas-catalog.md). They are not a size ranking: an identifier names a composition, so a publisher that spells overlays into the name carries far more of them than one that does not.

**Kind is asserted, not derived.** MIDAS carries no classification. It distinguishes these only accidentally, through whether the distribution half of a party segment names a utility or reads `XX` for unspecified, which is a good signal and not a definition. The mapping lives in `dev/list_california_parties.py`.

A community choice aggregator sells generation over an investor-owned utility's wires, so one of its customers is billed a composition: the aggregator's generation rate with the utility's delivery. Both halves have to be modeled to price a bill.

**IOU** investor-owned utility, **POU** publicly owned utility, **CCA** community choice aggregator. **Alert** and **GHG** are signals MIDAS carries that are not rates and have no seller.
