## Two axes, deliberately not collapsed

**Servable** answers: can a price server resolve this to a price? It is read from `dev/baseline/marginal-rates.edn` through the service-arrangement enumeration, over current versions only.

**Captures its source PDF** is `[modeling].complete` in the filing's own `tariff.toml`, which [LAYOUT.md §4.4](../LAYOUT.md) defines as *does this JSON-LD fully capture its source PDF(s)?* It is hand-curated by whoever modeled the filing against its source.

The two do not track each other and must not be collapsed. 1 of 71 filings carry `complete = true`, because the flag counts administrative provisions alongside pricing ones: a filing can be incomplete over a demand-meter threshold that changes nobody's bill, and a filing can be complete while getting a price wrong. LAYOUT.md §4.4 makes the general argument: *collapsing them into a single ready flag forces the modeler to pick one axis and lie about the other.*

## Caveats

- **As of the recorded baseline.** Servability is read from `dev/baseline/marginal-rates.edn`, 19 probe instants per key, resolved by clj-urpx 0.32.0. Nothing is re-resolved here.
- **Per-directory granularity.** A directory is one filing, not one version. It can hold a base RatePlan and the modifiers operative at its snapshot date, and a version node can appear in more than one directory.
- **Servable is not reviewed.** `[review].status` tracks whether a person has checked the filing, and neither column here reads it.
- **Modifier coverage is a separate axis again.** A base RatePlan being servable does not imply that every modifier that legally attaches to it has its own filing. See [`modifier-coverage.md`](./modifier-coverage.md).
