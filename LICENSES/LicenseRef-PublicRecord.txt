LicenseRef-PublicRecord — US Public Record

This license reference identifier denotes documents that are public record
under US federal or state law and are therefore not subject to copyright
restrictions on inclusion or redistribution.

The file(s) this identifier is attached to in REUSE.toml are regulatory
filings or published tariff schedules made available by a US utility or
regulatory body in the ordinary course of its public-facing duties. Such
documents are not the copyrightable creative expression of any party;
they are factual public records of regulatory processes (Public Utilities
Commission tariff filings, municipal utility rate schedules adopted by
city ordinance, etc.).

Under US copyright doctrine, factual public records of governmental
processes do not attract copyright protection. The `SPDX-FileCopyrightText`
attached to such files in REUSE.toml names the issuing utility for
attribution / provenance purposes only — it does not represent a
copyright assertion by that utility, nor by this repository.

No warranty is made by this repository's authors regarding the accuracy,
completeness, or currency of any public-record document included here.
Users who require an authoritative copy must consult the issuing utility
or regulator directly.

This LicenseRef identifier is provided per the SPDX specification so that
automated license scanners (ScanCode, FOSSology, REUSE) can cleanly
classify these files as non-copyrightable public-record content rather
than flagging them as unlicensed.

The wording of this LicenseRef closely follows the same identifier as
used by `urpx-org/urpx`, by convention, so that downstream users
classifying mixed urpx-org + grid-coordination repositories see a
consistent label for public-record content.
