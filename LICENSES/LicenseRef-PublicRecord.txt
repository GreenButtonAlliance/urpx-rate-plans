LicenseRef-PublicRecord - US Public Record

This license reference identifier denotes public-record documents:
regulatory filings or published tariff schedules made available by a US
utility or regulatory body in the ordinary course of its public-facing
duties (Public Utilities Commission tariff filings, municipal utility
rate schedules adopted by city ordinance, etc.).

This repository claims no copyright in these documents and makes no
determination about whether copyright subsists in them or about any
rights the issuing utility or regulator may hold. The
`SPDX-FileCopyrightText` attached to such files in REUSE.toml names the
issuing utility for provenance only; it is not a copyright assertion by
this repository.

No warranty is made by this repository's authors regarding the accuracy,
completeness, or currency of any public-record document included here.
Users who require an authoritative copy must consult the issuing utility
or regulator directly.

This LicenseRef identifier is provided per the SPDX specification so that
automated license scanners (ScanCode, FOSSology, REUSE) can classify these
files as public-record documents rather than flagging them as unlicensed.

`urpx-org/urpx` uses the same identifier for the same class of document,
so downstream users classifying mixed urpx-org and grid-coordination
repositories see a consistent label for public-record content.
